# Case Study Template

## Project Summary

What was built, who it helps, and why AI is useful.

## User Problem

Describe the painful decision or workflow in plain language.

## Target User

Who this is for and what situation they are in.

## Product Bet

What hypothesis the MVP tests.

## Core Flow

List the user journey from first input to useful outcome.

## AI Design

- model task
- structured inputs
- structured outputs
- validation
- fallback behavior
- uncertainty handling

## Guardrails

What the product refuses, softens, or asks follow-up questions about.

## Evals

What cases were tested and what good behavior looks like.

## Tradeoffs

What was intentionally cut, delayed, or simplified.

## Outcome

Demo status, metrics if available, and what changed after testing.

## What I Learned

Product lesson, AI lesson, technical lesson.

## Next Iteration

The smallest useful improvement after MVP.
