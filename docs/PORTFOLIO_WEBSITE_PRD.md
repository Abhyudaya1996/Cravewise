# PRD: Portfolio Website

## Problem

A hiring manager needs to understand quickly whether this portfolio proves real AI PM capability or just surface-level AI enthusiasm.

## Target User

AI product leaders, fintech PM managers, founders, and collaborators reviewing practical AI product work.

## JTBD

When I visit the portfolio, I want to see working demos, clear product judgment, and concrete AI guardrails so I can judge whether the builder can ship trustworthy AI products.

## MVP Scope

- Homepage with positioning and project cards
- Project pages for CraveWise, Financial Copilot, and PM Simulator
- Case study links
- Evidence sections: problem, workflow, AI design, guardrails, evals, demo status
- About page focused on fintech PM to AI-native PM transition

## Out Of Scope

- Blog CMS
- analytics dashboards
- authentication
- contact backend
- animation-heavy marketing site

## Success Metrics

- Reviewer can understand the portfolio thesis in under one minute.
- Each project page links to demo, docs, evals, and case study.
- Site explains constraints and tradeoffs, not only screenshots.
